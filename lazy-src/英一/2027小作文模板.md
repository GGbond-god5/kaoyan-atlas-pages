# 2027 英语一小作文模板（应用文 10 分）

> 以下是按公开考试要求整理的原创框架，不是官方答案。考场要先读题、圈出全部要点，再选框架，不要整段照搬。

## 一、阅卷先看什么

1. **内容要点**：题目要求写几点，就覆盖几点；漏一点通常直接掉档。
2. **格式与语域**：称呼、落款、正式/非正式语气是否匹配。
3. **语言准确**：时态、主谓一致、单复数、拼写。
4. **连贯**：句间有连接，段落清楚，不堆砌套话。

## 二、通用三段结构

| 段落 | 作用 | 篇幅 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 第一段 | 称呼 + 说明写信目的 | 1–2 句 |
| 第二段 | 主体：2–3 个要点，逐条展开 | 4–6 句 |
| 第三段 | 礼貌收尾 + 期待回复 + 署名 | 1–2 句 |

开头目的句（任选，不要每篇都用同一句）：

- I am writing to suggest that …
- I am writing to recommend … to you.
- I am writing to invite you to …
- I am writing to apologize for …
- I am writing to apply for …

结尾句：

- I would appreciate it if you could take my suggestion into consideration.
- I hope you will find this recommendation helpful.
- I look forward to your reply at your earliest convenience.
- Please accept my sincere apology again.

## 三、按题型准备

### 1. 建议信

**开头**：I am writing to share some suggestions regarding …

**主体句块**：

- First and foremost, it would be advisable to …
- In addition, consideration should be given to …
- It would also be helpful if …

**收尾**：I hope these suggestions will be of some help.

**易错点**：只写建议、不写理由；语气过硬（应使用 would / could / it might be better to）。

### 2. 推荐信

**开头**：I am writing to recommend … to you.

**主体句块**：

- The main reason for my recommendation is that …
- What makes it stand out is …
- It is particularly suitable for you because …

**收尾**：I am convinced that you will benefit a lot from it.

**易错点**：推荐对象和自己经历脱节；只夸不举具体例子。

### 3. 邀请信

**开头**：I am writing to invite you to …

**主体句块**：

- The event is scheduled to take place in … on …
- It would be a great pleasure if you could join us.
- Please let me know whether you are available.

**收尾**：I am looking forward to your presence.

**易错点**：时间、地点、活动内容三要素缺一；忘记询问对方是否方便。

### 4. 道歉信

**开头**：I am writing to express my sincere apology for …

**主体句块**：

- The reason why I failed to … is that …
- To make up for it, I would like to …
- I fully understand the inconvenience I have caused.

**收尾**：Please accept my apology once again.

**易错点**：只道歉不补救；把责任推给对方。

### 5. 通知 / 告示

**格式**：标题（Notice）+ 正文 + 落款（组织 + 日期）。

**主体句块**：

- A … will be held in … on …
- Those who are interested are welcome to sign up before …
- For further information, please contact …

**易错点**：写成私人信件；缺报名方式或截止时间。

### 6. 申请信

**开头**：I am writing to apply for …

**主体句块**：

- I believe I am qualified for this position because …
- My experience in … has equipped me with …
- I would be grateful if you could offer me an opportunity.

**收尾**：Thank you for your consideration.

**易错点**：只写愿望、不写能力证据；语气过于随意。

## 四、可替换的连接与语气

- 递进：first of all / in addition / moreover / what is more
- 转折：however / nevertheless / on second thought
- 因果：therefore / as a result / consequently
- 礼貌：I would appreciate it if … / It would be kind of you to …

## 五、常见扣分点

1. 称呼写错：给朋友用 Dear Sir or Madam，或给机构用 Dear Tom。
2. 署名写成中文或真实姓名；题目没要求时用 Li Ming。
3. 要点漏写；尤其“说明原因”“提出建议”“表达感谢”这类动词。
4. 全篇简单句堆叠，没有连接。
5. 字数明显不足（小作文通常 100 词左右）。

## 六、考前检查清单

- [ ] 题目要求的要点是否逐条覆盖？
- [ ] 称呼、落款、语气是否匹配对象？
- [ ] 每段是否有明确中心句？
- [ ] 时态是否统一（尤其邀请、道歉、通知）？
- [ ] 是否出现拼写、主谓一致、单复数错误？
- [ ] 是否留出 2 分钟通读并改错？
