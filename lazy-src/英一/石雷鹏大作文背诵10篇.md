### 范文

This is a table, clearly illustrating the changes in the domestic sales of gasoline-powered and new-energy vehicles in China during the first half of 2025. In this period, the sales of new-energy cars increased steadily, from 0.79 million in January to 1.12 million in June. By contrast, the sales of gasoline cars fluctuated around 1.1 million. It is particularly noteworthy that the sales of new-energy vehicles surpassed those of gasoline-powered vehicles in May.

Such statistics, apparently, can be associated with the fierce competition between new-energy vehicles and traditional cars. Then, what resulted in the phenomenon above? For one thing, in recent years, China has been characterized by its boom and prosperity in economy, which has enabled a host of families to possess additional income to purchase gasoline-powered or new-energy cars. For another, compared with traditional cars, new-energy vehicles are much cheaper, more eco-friendly, more efficient and require less maintenance. Thus, an increasing number of people have chosen new-energy cars.

To my understanding, China's car manufacturers are supposed to pay more attention to the new-energy car market due to its huge potential. Meanwhile, it is advisable for them to work out comprehensive strategies to develop more types of new-energy vehicles, such as solar-powered cars. (203 words)

### 参考译文

这是一张表格，清晰展示了2025年上半年中国燃油车和新能源汽车国内销量的变化。在此期间，新能源汽车的销量稳步上升，从1月的79万辆增加到6月的112万辆。相比之下，燃油车的销量在110万辆上下波动。尤其值得注意的是，新能源汽车的销量在5月份超过了燃油车。

显然，这样的数据能让人联想到新能源汽车和传统汽车之间的激烈竞争。那么，出现上述现象的原因是什么？一方面，近年来，中国的典型特征在于其经济的繁荣发展，这使得很多家庭有了额外的收入来购买燃油车或新能源汽车。另一方面，与传统汽车相比，新能源汽车更加便宜、环保、高效，需要的保养也更少。因此，越来越多的人选择了新能源汽车。

在我看来，中国的汽车制造商应该更关注新能源汽车市场，因为其潜力巨大。同时，他们还应制定综合策略来开发更多种类的新能源汽车，比如太阳能汽车。

### 范文

Presented is a bar chart, which clearly illustrates the striking contrasts in employed people's purposes for further study in a certain city in 2025. To be specific, enhancing current job skills ranked first, accounting for 65%. The next was acquiring knowledge in new areas, at 48%, followed by developing personal interests at 37%. Finally came gaining diplomas or vocational certificates, at 26%.

Such statistics, definitely, can be associated with the growing significance of continuing to learn after starting one's career. To begin with, contemporary society is characterized by fierce competition and rapid technological advances. This phenomenon is particularly worth attention for the reason that it is the rapid development of technology that causes people to have no option but to keep learning for the purpose of adapting to job requirements. A case in point is AI, as it is transforming many fields. In addition, compared to the past, employed people today possess better financial conditions to pursue their interests or make up for deficiencies in academic qualifications.

In the final analysis, I am convinced that it is advisable for everyone to develop the habit and awareness of lifelong learning, which can give us a competitive edge, inspire our passion for striving, and help us seize opportunities. (206 words)

总而言之，我认为每个人都有必要培养终身学习的习惯和意识。终身学习能够给我们带来竞争优势，激发我们奋斗的热情，并帮助我们抓住机遇。

## 大作文03 图画＋图表：公共场所无障碍通道的普及

### 题目要求

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the picture and the chart below. In your essay, you should

(1) describe the picture and the chart briefly,

(2) interpret the implied meaning, and

(3) give your comments.

Write your answer in 160-200 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)

图画内容：左图为一栋公共建筑的入口，入口旁设有无障碍坡道，一位坐轮椅的残障人士正沿着坡道轻松进入建筑。

图表内容：右图为柱状图，标题为“2021～2025年某市公共场所设置无障碍通道的比例”。纵轴为百分比（0、20%、40%、60%、80%、100%），横轴为年份。各年份数据为：2021年 56%，2022年 64%，2023年 71%，2024年 79%，2025年 85%，呈逐年上升趋势。图注：无障碍通道指坡道、升降设施或其他便于行动不便者进出的通道。

### 题目解读

2024年考研英语（一）大作文采用了“图画+图表”的形式，题目给出一张图画和一张图表，要求考生简述图画、图表内容并提出见解。这是考研英语（一）第一次采用这种形式，看似复杂，却也在考纲描述的范围之内。根据考试大纲，英语（一）大作文提供信息的形式包含文字、图画、图表等。遇到此类作文题时，大家不必担心，只要利用本书范文展示的功能句写作，就可以事半功倍。

写作时，在首段先对图画细节进行描述，然后概述图表的主要内容及显著特征，比如占比大小、增减趋势等。在中间段，结合图画及图表内容联系主旨，或简要分析导致这种现象或趋势的原因，或阐述其积极意义，也可举例说明。最后在尾段进行总结，表达个人观点，提出建议或发出号召。相关功能句可参考后面的范文。

个人观点＋号召

1. disabled adj. 丧失能力的，有残疾的

### 范文

The left drawing shows a simple but quite warm scene, in which there is a disabled person, using a wheelchair to move easily along an accessible pathway. Meanwhile, the right chart clearly illustrates that in a certain city, from 2021 to 2025, the proportion of public places equipped with accessible pathways increased remarkably, from 56% to 85%.

The scene and statistics above, apparently, can be associated with the special care for people with disabilities in a harmonious society. Undoubtedly, in recent years, China has been characterized by its economic boom and prosperity, which has enabled the government to devote more financial budgets to public facilities, including accessible pathways. This phenomenon is particularly worth attention for the reason that it is these facilities that enable people with limited mobility to enjoy tremendous convenience and get around independently. Another case in point is that on many buses, there are seats reserved for the elderly, children, the sick, people with disabilities, and expectant mothers. To my mind, this special care can be likened to sunshine, which can bring warmth to those in need, inspire their passion for life, and help us build a civilized society.

In the final analysis, I deem that society as a whole is supposed to foster a positive atmosphere to care for, love, and protect those with disabilities and the like. (222 words)

### 参考译文

显然，上述场景和数据能让人联想到和谐社会中对残障人士的特殊关爱。毫无疑问，近年来，中国的特点在于其经济繁荣昌盛，这使政府能够投入更多财政预算，用在包括无障碍通道在内的公共设施上。这一现象尤其值得关注，因为正是这些设施让行动不便的人获得巨大便利，能够独立出行。另一个典型例子是，很多公交车上都设有老幼病残孕专座。在我看来，这种特殊关爱可以比作阳光，它能给有需要的人带来温暖，激发他们对生活的热情，并帮助我们建设文明社会。

总而言之，我认为全社会都应营造积极氛围，去关心、关爱并保护残障人士以及其他同类群体。

### 范文

In the left drawing, there is a professional healthcare worker, imparting first aid knowledge to two youngsters. Meanwhile, the right chart clearly illustrates that from 2021 to 2025, in a certain city, the proportion of residents aged 18 to 60 who attended first aid courses ascended significantly, from 9% to 32%.

The scene and statistics above, definitely, can be associated with a positive shift in public awareness regarding safety and health. Undoubtedly, in recent decades, China has been characterized by its economic prosperity and technological advancement, which have enabled people to enjoy a higher quality of life and have more opportunities to acquire various kinds of knowledge, including first aid skills. This phenomenon is particularly worth attention for the reason that it is mastering basic first aid skills that enables citizens to save their own or others' lives in emergencies, such as cardiac arrests, accidents and the like. Thanks to such knowledge, I helped save an injured man after a car accident.

In a nutshell, I am convinced that it is advisable for hospitals to offer more free training courses to people from all walks of life. Meanwhile, society as a whole should foster a positive atmosphere to inspire the public to learn these vital skills. (238 words)

### 参考译文

左图中，一名专业医护人员正在向两名年轻人传授急救知识。与此同时，右图清楚地显示，从2021年到2025年，某市18～60岁居民中参加过急救课程的比例显著上升，从9%提高到了32%。

显然，上述场景和数据能让人联想到公众在安全与健康意识方面的积极转变。毫无疑问，近几十年来，中国的特点是经济繁荣和科技进步，这使人们能够享受更高质量的生活，并有更多机会获取包括急救技能在内的各种知识。这一现象尤其值得关注，因为掌握基础急救技能能够使居民在心脏骤停、意外事故等紧急情况下挽救自己或他人的生命。我就是一个典型的例子。作为年轻人，我喜欢观看健康教育视频和电视节目，也喜欢参加医学讲座，以学习急救知识。多亏了那些知识，我曾在一次车祸后救助了一名伤者。

总而言之，我认为医院应该向各行各业的人提供更多免费培训课程。同时，整个社会也应营造积极的氛围，鼓励公众学习这些至关重要的技能。

## 大作文10 材料作文：奖励式教育（新大纲）

### 题目要求

**Directions:**

Read the following excerpt from an article and write an essay. In your essay, you should explain whether or to what extent you agree with the author. Support your argument with reasons and relevant examples.

Write your answer in 160-200 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (20 points)

材料：

Many parents look for some sort of reward system that might help improve their children's behaviors at home. While this incentive-based parenting can be a quick fix, research clearly indicates that it can become a problem over time. One problem is that children often do not develop an internal motivation needed to maintain behaviors when the reward is not being offered. For example, if you reward your children every time they receive an "A" on a test, their desire to learn will depend on whether there is a prize. As a result, it becomes very difficult for them to develop interest in learning itself, and the internal motivation to learn.

### 题目解读

#### 材料译文

许多父母都在寻找某种可能有助于改善孩子居家表现的奖励机制。尽管这种奖励式养育可作应急之用，但研究表明，随着时间的推移，可能会引发问题。一个问题是，当没有奖励时，孩子们通常无法形成保持行为所需的内在动力。例如，如果每当你的孩子在考试中得了A，你就奖励他们，那么他们学习的动力将取决于有无奖励。这样一来，他们就很难培养对学习本身的兴趣和形成学习的内在动力。

本题属于材料作文，2027年新大纲给出了相关样题，要求考生针对一段阅读材料进行写作，此前从未考查过。即使今年考查到，大家也不必担心，前面图画作文中的很多功能句同样适用。对于此类题目，大家在写作时可先在第一段对节选内容进行概述，进而表明自己的观点（同意或反对），然后在第二段进行论证（陈述原因、举例说明），最后进行总结即可。
