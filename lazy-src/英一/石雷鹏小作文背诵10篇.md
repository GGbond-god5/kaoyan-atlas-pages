# 第一章 小作文话题预测

## 小作文01 通知/告示：介绍＋邀请

### 题目要求

**Directions:**

Suppose your university is going to host a welcome party for new international students. Write a notice (or an announcement) in the name of the Students' Union to

(1) briefly introduce the party, and

(2) invite the international students to attend it.

Write your answer in about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

**Do not** use your own name in your notice; use "The Students' Union" instead. (10 points)

### 范文

Notice/Announcement

Friday, June 10

**It is so great to**① hear that you, international students, have arrived at our university. This notice/announcement **is for the purpose of** inviting you to attend a welcome party.

To begin with, the party **is scheduled to start** at 19:00 on June 20 in the Center of Students' Activities **and last for** approximately two hours. In addition, this party **is characterized by** a variety of interesting activities, **including** singing, dancing, Chinese culture introduction, and fun competitions. Of course, some traditional Chinese snacks will be served at the party. To conclude, **it is advisable for** you **to** participate in this party, which **will bring you** opportunities to make friends, **arouse your passion for** the campus life, **and help you** know China better.

Finally, **we are looking forward to** your participation.

The Students' Union

(136 words)

> ① 全书参考范文中加粗的内容为功能句。

### 参考译文

通知/告示

6月10日，星期五

很高兴得知各位留学生已经抵校。本通知/告示旨在邀请各位参加欢迎会。

首先，欢迎会定于6月20日19:00在学生活动中心举行，时长约2小时。此外，这次欢迎会的特色是活动精彩丰富，包括唱歌、跳舞、中国文化介绍和趣味比赛。当然，欢迎会上还会提供一些中国传统小吃。总之，建议你们参加这次欢迎会，它将带给你们结交新朋友的机会，激发你们对校园生活的热情，并帮助你们更好地了解中国。

最后，期待你们的参与。

学生会

### 词汇预习

1. attend v. 参加，出席
2. schedule v. 为……安排时间
3. approximately adv. 大约
4. characterize v. 使……具有特点
5. a variety of 各种各样的，多种的
6. snack n. 点心，小吃
7. advisable adj. 明智的，可取的
8. participate v. 参加（派 participation n. 参加）
9. opportunity n. 机会
10. arouse v. 激起，引起
11. campus n.（大学）校园，校区

## 小作文02 邮件：建议＋说明

### 题目要求

**Directions:**

Suppose a foreign friend of yours has been looking for AI tools to enhance learning efficiency. Write him/her an e-mail to make a recommendation and explain your reasons.

Write your answer in about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

**Do not** use your own name in your e-mail; use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)

### 范文

Dear Sky,

**I am quite pleased to** hear that you are looking for AI tools to promote learning efficiency. Today, **I am writing to** make a recommendation.

**It is advisable for** you **to** try out several AI tools in learning since **it is** by trying them out **that** you can know which one can best suit your needs. **A case in point is** myself. **As** a senior **who** is preparing for the national entrance exams for postgraduate studies, **I** tried ERNIE Bot, Tongyi, and Kimi. In the end, I chose Kimi, which is **characterized by** its excellent performance in information collection. This AI tool **can bring me** high efficiency, **inspire my passion for** learning, **and help me** save time and energy.

**I am looking forward to** discussing more details of the AI tools after you use them.

Best wishes,
Li Ming

(141 words)

### 参考译文

亲爱的斯凯：

很高兴得知你在寻找AI工具来提高学习效率。今天我写信来是想做个推荐。

建议你在学习中尝试几个AI工具，因为通过试用这些工具，你能够知道哪个最符合你的需求。一个典型的例子就是我自己。作为一名正在准备全国研究生入学考试的大四学生，我试过文心一言、通义和Kimi。最终，我选择了Kimi，它的特点是在信息搜集方面表现出色。这个AI工具能够让我更高效，激发我对学习的热情，并帮助我节省时间和精力。

等你试用之后，我期待跟你探讨AI工具的更多细节。

祝好
李明

### 词汇预习

1. promote v. 增进，促进
2. efficiency n. 效率
3. recommendation n. 推荐
4. try out 试用，试验
5. suit one's needs 满足某人的需要
6. a case in point 恰当的例证
7. in the end 最终
8. excellent adj. 优秀的，极好的
9. performance n. 表现

## 小作文03 邮件：介绍＋建议

### 题目要求

**Directions:**

Suppose you are a senior student. Read the following e-mail from a first-year student in your department and write a reply.

> Dear Li Ming,
>
> I've heard that our university has launched a peer mentoring program for new students, but I don't know much about it. Could you give me more details?
>
> Yours,
> Michael

Write your answer in about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

**Do not** use your own name in your e-mail; use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)

### 范文

Dear Michael,

**I'm quite pleased to** know that you are interested in the peer mentoring program for new students. Today, **I am writing for the purpose of** introducing certain details.

This program pairs freshmen with seniors to help them adapt to college life. To begin with, college life **is characterized by** the fact that students have more spare time. Then, **compared with** high schools, there are a **wider** variety of campus activities, clubs and organizations in college. Thus we, **as** senior students, **are supposed to** help you develop a new pace of study and life suited for college. To conclude, **it's advisable for** you **to** sign up for the peer mentoring program. **I'm convinced that** you will benefit a lot from it.

Of course, if you have any other questions, please feel free to ask me.

Yours truly,
Li Ming

(140 words)

### 参考译文

亲爱的迈克尔：

得知你对面向新生的同伴指导项目感兴趣，我十分高兴。今天我写这封信是想介绍一些相关细节。

本项目将新生与高年级学生结对，来帮助他们适应大学生活。首先，大学生活的特点是学生拥有更多空闲时间。其次，与高中相比，大学里有更加丰富多样的校园活动、社团和学生组织。因此，作为高年级学生，我们应当帮助你们形成一套新的、适合大学的学习和生活节奏。总之，建议你报名参加同伴指导项目。我相信你将会从中获益良多。

当然，如果你还有其他问题，随时可以问我。

谨上
李明

### 词汇预习

1. mentor v. 指导，培训
2. adapt to 适应（新情况）
3. spare adj. 空闲的
4. develop v. 获得，形成
5. sign up for 报名参加

## 小作文06 邮件：道歉＋建议

### 题目要求

**Directions:**

Suppose your foreign friend James is coming to China this Saturday. You originally planned to pick him up at the airport, but could not make it now. Write him an e-mail to

(1) make an apology, and

(2) propose a solution.

Write your answer in about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

**Do not** use your own name in your e-mail; use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)

### 范文

Dear James,

**I am quite pleased to** hear that you are coming to China and I originally planned to pick you up at the airport. However, **I am writing for the purpose of** making an apology since I am afraid I can't make it.

You know that during the past half year, **I have been exceedingly busy because I allocated nearly all my time and energy for** preparing for the national entrance exams for postgraduate studies, which is of great significance to me. Then, due to my carelessness, I did not notice that the date of the test is the day when you will arrive in China until two minutes ago. Accordingly, I have no option but to ask my best friend, Sky, to pick you up at the airport this Saturday.

**I am deeply sorry** and I plan to show you around Beijing after the test.

Best wishes,
Li Ming

(151 words)

### 参考译文

亲爱的詹姆斯：

很高兴得知你将来到中国，我原本计划去机场接你。但是，我写信来是想道歉，因为我恐怕去不了了。

你知道我过去半年特别忙，因为我几乎把所有时间和精力都用在了准备全国研究生入学考试上，这个考试对我来说非常重要。而且，由于我的粗心，直到两分钟前我才意识到考试当天就是你抵达中国的那天。因此，我别无选择，只能请我最好的朋友斯凯本周六去机场接你。

真的很抱歉。等考完试以后，我计划带你在北京四处逛逛。

祝好
李明

## 小作文09 邮件：建议＋鼓励

### 题目要求

**Directions:**

Suppose your friend Jack did not do well in his maths midterm exam. He wrote you an e-mail asking for advice. Write him a reply to

(1) give your advice, and

(2) encourage him.

Write your answer in about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

**Do not** use your own name in your e-mail; use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)

### 范文

Dear Jack,

**I am deeply sorry to** hear that you did not do well in your recent maths midterm exam. Today, **I am writing for the purpose of** sharing my suggestions and encouraging you.

To begin with, **it is advisable for** you **to** prepare in advance since **it is** full preparation **that enables** you **to** accumulate adequate knowledge and skills for the test. **A case in point is** myself. **Compared with** others, I am **not smarter**, but full preparation **can bring me** confidence, **arouse my passion for** learning, **and help me** succeed in maths tests. In addition, **it is of great necessity for** you **to** summarize your errors as success derives from failure.

To conclude, **I am convinced that if you do your utmost to** prepare and practice in advance, you will do better in the next exam.

Best wishes,
Li Ming

(142 words)

### 参考译文

亲爱的杰克：

很遗憾得知最近的数学期中考试你没考好。今天，我写信来是想分享一些我的建议并给你一些鼓励。

首先，建议你提前准备，因为正是充分的准备使你能够积累足够的知识和考试技巧。一个典型的例子就是我自己。与其他人相比，我不算聪明，但充分的准备能够带给我自信，唤起我对学习的热情，并帮助我在数学考试中获得成功。此外，你还有必要总结自己的错误，因为失败是成功之母。

总之，我坚信，你如果竭尽全力提前准备和练习，下次会考得更好。

祝好
李明

2. in advance 预先，事先
3. necessity n. 必要，需要
4. summarize v. 总结

## 小作文10 会议纪要（新大纲）

### 题目要求

**Directions:**

Suppose your class held a meeting to discuss how to prevent food waste in daily life. Please write the minutes for the meeting. Your minutes should include

(1) basic information about the discussion, and

(2) issues and solutions raised in the discussion.

Write your answer in about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

**Do not** use your own name in your minutes; use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)

### 题目解读

纪要一般指会议纪要，是2027年大纲中提到的小作文考查形式之一。在写作时，一定要先看清题目要求写哪些内容。通常情况下，一篇纪要需要包含会议的基本信息，如时间、地点、参会人员、会议主题、会议内容等，会议内容可能涉及会议各环节做了什么、最终得出什么结论等。

这种题型看上去很新，但实际上在写作时，仍旧可以大量运用功能句。如果考试考到了，大家也不用担心，只需审好题，并参考下面范文的思路写作即可。

### 范文

Minutes

This meeting **was for the purpose of** discussing how to prevent food waste in everyday life and finally all participants came to the same conclusion.

The meeting **began** at 14:00 on June 20 in Classroom 201 **and lasted for** two hours. Professor Sky raised the question and pointed out that **compared to** the past years, we enjoy a **richer** life and thus an increasing number of youngsters do not cherish food. Then, all classmates agreed that this phenomenon **is particularly worth attention**. **We deemed that it is of great necessity for** us **to** stop wasting not only food, but also time, electricity, water, and the like.

The conclusion is that we **are supposed to** take immediate actions to prevent food waste in daily life, **such as** only buying what we need and not throwing away leftovers.

Minutes recorder: Li Ming

(141 words)

### 参考译文

会议纪要

本次会议旨在讨论如何在日常生活中避免食物浪费，最终所有与会人员得出一致结论。

会议于6月20日14:00在201教室举行，持续时长为2小时。斯凯教授提出了这一问题，并指出，与过去相比，我们的生活更加富裕，于是越来越多的年轻人不珍惜食物。接着，全班同学一致同意这一现象非常值得关注。大家认为，我们不仅很有必要杜绝食物浪费，还要避免浪费时间、电、水以及其他类似资源。

结论是，我们应该立刻采取行动来避免日常生活中的食物浪费，例如只买需要的，以及不扔掉剩饭剩菜。

记录人：李明

### 词汇预习

1. minute n. [~s] 会议记录
2. participant n. 参加者
3. come to a conclusion 得出结论
4. raise v. 提起，提出
5. cherish v. 珍惜
6. phenomenon n. 现象
7. like n. 类似的人（或物）
8. immediate adj. 立刻的
9. leftover n. [常用复数] 吃剩的饭菜
