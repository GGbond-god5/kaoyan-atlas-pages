# 第四章 写作高频词汇和表达背诵

> 来源：《石雷鹏作文冲刺背诵20篇（2027 考研英语一）》第四章（Chapter 4），PDF 第 126–135 页（标题页 126，正文印刷页 122–130）。

## 01 学校活动与教育

### 活动相关

1. spare time 课余时间，业余时间
2. participate in extra-curricular activities 参加课外活动
3. debate competition 辩论赛
4. arrange/organize a conference 安排/组织会议
5. recruit volunteers 招募志愿者
6. apply for volunteer work 申请志愿工作
7. relevant information 相关信息

### 通知、计划相关

1. on behalf of the Students' Union 代表学生会
2. inform you of the time and place 将时间和地点告诉你
3. seek for advice 寻求建议
4. reply at your earliest convenience 尽早回复
5. receive your favorable reply 收到您的肯定答复
6. cancel the plan 取消计划
7. let me know in advance 提前告诉我

### 知识、教育相关

1. lifelong education 终身教育
2. acquire knowledge 获取知识
3. theoretical knowledge 理论知识
4. practical skills 实用技能
5. read extensively 广泛阅读
6. shape one's character 塑造性格
7. stimulate/stifle creativity 激发/扼杀创造力
8. foster creative/critical thinking 培养创造性/批判性思维
9. comprehensive development 全面发展
10. contribute to one's success 有助于某人的成功
11. accumulate ability and experience 积累能力和经验

### 学校、学习相关

1. enroll new students 录取新生
2. college admission requirements 大学录取条件
3. afford tuition 付得起学费
4. boost academic achievement 提升学业成就
5. increase/reduce the burden of learning 加重/减轻学习负担
6. the national entrance exams for postgraduate studies 全国研究生入学考试
7. pursue a master's degree 攻读硕士学位（pursue v. 追求）
8. university graduate 大学毕业生
9. pass/fail an exam 通过考试/考试不及格

## 02 表达个人情感

1. be fond of 喜欢
2. be keen on 对……有兴趣
3. be disappointed with 对……很失望
4. extend/express my gratitude to sb. 向某人表达我的感谢（extend v. 表达）
5. be grateful to sb. for sth. 感谢某人做了某事
6. make a complaint 投诉
7. apologize for being late 为迟到道歉
8. demand/receive an apology 要求/接受道歉
9. apologize for any inconvenience caused 为带来的不便致歉
10. We regret to inform you that ... 我们很遗憾地通知您……

## 03 道德品质

### 行为、品质相关

1. foster the sense of teamwork 培养团队意识
2. strengthen the cooperation 加强合作
3. damage the credibility of ... 损害……的信誉
4. require patience and perseverance 需要耐心和毅力
5. strive for the best 精益求精
6. selfless devotion to ... 对……的无私奉献
7. tireless effort 孜孜不倦的努力
8. take action 付诸行动
9. conquer hardships and adversities 克服困难和逆境
10. give up halfway 半途而废
11. honesty and integrity 诚信
12. build/boost one's self-confidence 增强/提高自信心
13. remain optimistic 保持乐观
14. get rid of pessimistic mood 摆脱悲观情绪
15. gather the courage to do sth. 鼓起勇气做某事
16. be prudent in making decisions 谨慎做决定
17. down-to-earth advice 务实的建议
18. Haste makes waste. 欲速则不达。

### 道德、责任相关

1. uphold/violate public morality 维护/违反公共道德
2. comply with/observe the public policy 遵守公共政策
3. spitting/littering in public places 随地吐痰/乱扔垃圾
4. lack of morality 道德缺失
5. follow moral values 遵循道德价值观
6. shoulder/undertake one's responsibility 承担责任
7. avoid one's responsibility 逃避责任
8. make/keep/break a promise 许下/信守/违背诺言
9. rights and interests 权益

1. a well-off family 小康之家

1. air pollution 空气污染
2. vehicle exhaust emission 机动车尾气排放
3. wastewater discharge 污水排放
4. habitat degradation 栖息地恶化
5. industrial waste 工业废料
6. household garbage 生活垃圾

8. a plastic bag 塑料袋
9. environment-friendly products 环保产品

## 08 数据变化

### 上升

1. the ratio surged from 31.7% to 73.5% 该比例从31.7%飙升至73.5%
2. show an upward trend in exports 出口量呈现上升趋势
3. show an increase in prices 物价呈现上涨趋势
4. display a remarkable rise 表现出明显的上升

### 下降

1. the ratio plunged from 73.5% to 31.7% 该比例从73.5%骤降至31.7%
2. a slump in prices 物价暴跌
3. maintain a downward trend 保持下降趋势
4. drop steeply 急剧下降

### 平稳

1. Sales leveled off after a period of sharp growth. 销售额快速增长了一段时间后趋于平稳。
2. the ratio remains stable at around 70% 该比例保持在70%左右

## 09 通用衔接词

1. 综上所述 in conclusion、to summarize/conclude、on the whole、all in all
2. 总的来说 overall、generally speaking
3. 简而言之 in a nutshell、in brief
4. 因为 for the reason that（+句子）、owing to / due to（+名词短语）
5. 尽管 despite that（+句子）、in spite of（+名词短语）
6. 假如 provided that（+句子）
7. 关于 as regards（+名词短语）、with respect to（+名词短语）
8. 首先……其次……最后…… Firstly, ... Secondly, ... Finally, ... / To begin with, ... In addition, ... Finally, ...
9. 一方面……另一方面…… On the one hand, ... On the other hand, ... / For one thing, ... For another, ...
10. 因此 therefore、hence、thus、as a result、consequently、accordingly
11. 事实上 in reality、as a matter of fact、in fact
12. 此外 besides、moreover、in addition、what's more
13. 在我看来 to my understanding、from my perspective
