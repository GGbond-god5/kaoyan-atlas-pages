## 小作文必背功能句

### 1. 自我介绍/个人情况

- As a senior student at Peking University who is quite interested in cultural communication, I ...
  作为北京大学的一名大四学生，我对文化交流非常感兴趣，我……

### 2. 寒暄

- It is so great to/So glad to hear that you, international students, have arrived at our university.
  很高兴得知各位留学生已经抵校。
- I am very/quite happy/pleased to hear that you are looking for AI tools to promote learning efficiency.
  很高兴得知你在寻找 AI 工具来提高学习效率。
- I am deeply sorry to hear that you did not do well in your recent maths midterm exam.
  很遗憾得知最近的数学期中考试你没考好。

### 3. （写信/通知等）目的

- This notice/announcement is for the purpose of inviting you to attend a welcome party.
  本通知/告示旨在邀请各位参加欢迎会。
- I am writing to suggest a topic.
  我写信来是想推荐一个主题。
- I am writing for the purpose of making an apology since I am afraid I can't make it.
  我写信来是想道歉，因为我恐怕去不了了。

### 4. 建议

- It is advisable for you to participate in this welcome party.
  建议你们参加这次欢迎会。
- We are supposed to take immediate actions to prevent food waste in daily life.
  我们应该立刻采取行动来避免日常生活中的食物浪费。
- I suggest that you prepare in advance.
  我建议你提前准备。
- It is exceedingly/very necessary for you to lower the price.
  很有必要降低价格。
  注：exceedingly/very necessary 可替换为 of great necessity。

### 5. 原因（强调句）

- It was your tremendous help that enabled me to enjoy a comfortable and fruitful trip of learning.
  正是您的鼎力相助，使我得享一段舒适且获益良多的学习之旅。
  注：enable 可根据情况调整为 cause、allow。

### 6. 积极意义（排比句）

- Participating in relevant activities can bring you opportunities to enjoy diverse traditional foods, arouse your passion for Chinese culture, and help you gain a unique experience.
  参加相关活动能够让你有机会品尝各种传统美食，唤起你对中国文化的热情，并帮助你收获独特的体验。

### 7. 举例/列举

- China is characterized by its time-honored history and a variety of traditional festivals, such as the Spring Festival, the Mid-Autumn Day, and the Dragon Boat Festival.
  中国的特点是历史悠久且拥有各种各样的传统节日，例如春节、中秋节和端午节。
  注：such as 可根据情况调整为 including。
- A case in point is myself.
  一个典型的例子就是我自己。

### 8. 表达感谢/不满/意愿

- I am deeply/quite/much grateful to you for your help.
  我非常感激您的帮助。
- We are much/rather disappointed with the cafeteria staff.
  我们对食堂的工作人员非常失望。
- We're perfectly willing to participate.
  我们非常乐意参与。

### 9. 表达期待

- We are looking forward to your participation.
  期待你们的参与。
- Hope that they will improve their customer service.
  希望他们能改善客户服务。

### 10. 个人观点

- I am convinced that you will enjoy a comfortable and fruitful trip of Chinese culture.
  我坚信，你会拥有一次舒适的中国文化之旅，收获满满。
- I deem that it is advisable for you to visit in person the Great Wall, the Summer Palace, the Forbidden City and so on in your spare time.
  我觉得您可以在闲暇时亲自参观一下长城、颐和园和故宫等。

### 11. 对比

- Compared with/to others, I possess richer experience in communicating with foreigners.
  与其他人相比，我与外国人交流的经验更丰富。
  注：结构为：Compared with/to ..., ... 比较级（或含比较意义的表达）...

### 12. 典型特征

- In addition, this welcome party is characterized by a variety of interesting activities, including singing, dancing, Chinese culture introduction, and fun competitions.
  此外，这次欢迎会的特色是活动精彩丰富，包括唱歌、跳舞、中国文化介绍和趣味比赛。

### 13. 事件背景

- The welcome party is scheduled to start/begin at 19:00 on June 20 in the Center of Students' Activities and last for approximately two hours.
  欢迎会定于 6 月 20 日 19:00 在学生活动中心举行，时长约 2 小时。
  注：is scheduled to 也可写为 will 或 is going to。

### 14. 值得去做/关注

- These traditional Chinese snacks are particularly worth trying.
  这些中国传统小吃非常值得品尝。
- All classmates agreed that this phenomenon is particularly worth attention.
  全班同学一致同意这一现象非常值得关注。

### 15. 说明自己忙碌

- You know that during the past half year, I have been exceedingly busy because/since I allocated nearly all my time and energy for preparing for the national entrance exams for postgraduate studies.
  你知道我过去半年特别忙，因为我几乎把所有时间和精力都用在了准备全国研究生入学考试上。
  注：allocated nearly all my time and energy for 可替换为 devoted nearly all my time and energy to 或 focused nearly all my attention on。

### 16. 展望未来

- I will do my utmost to fulfill my duties.
  我将尽最大努力履行我的职责。
- If you do your utmost to prepare and practice in advance, you will do better in the next exam.
  你如果竭尽全力提前准备和练习，下次会考得更好。

## 大作文必背功能句

### 1. 概述图画/材料

- These are two simple but satirical drawings.
  这是两幅简单但具有讽刺性的漫画。
  注：satirical 可根据情况改为 inspiring、illuminating、enlightening 等，drawing 可替换为 picture、cartoon。
- This is a simple but enlightening cartoon, in which there is a youngster, bending over to pick up a bottle on the ground.
  这是一幅简单但具有启发性的漫画，漫画中，有个年轻人正弯腰捡地上的一个瓶子。
- This is a simple but enlightening excerpt of an article, in which the author argues that incentive-based parenting can be effective at home for the short term, but will become a problem in the long run.
  这是一段简单但具有启发性的文章节选，作者在文中指出，短期来看，这种奖励式教育在家里是有效的，但从长远来看会引发问题。

### 2. 图画细节（时间—地点—人/物/事—行为—伴随）

- Beside him sits his son, with perplexity on his face, asking, "Dad, are you listening to me?"
  他的儿子坐在旁边，一脸困惑地问："爸爸，你在听我说话吗？"
  注：结构为：...（地点）verb（动作，如 sit）...（人/物）, v-ing ...（行为）, with ...（伴随特征）
- In front of an ancient building, there are two girls, discussing whether to enter it for a visit.
  在一座古建筑前，有两个女孩正在讨论是否进去参观。
  注：结构为：...（时间/地点）, there be ...（人/物/事）, v-ing ...（行为）, with ...（伴随特征）

### 3. 对比（+结果）

- Although the watch is already made, he does not seem satisfied.
  尽管手表已经组装好，但他看起来还是不满意。
- However, compared with traditional courses, there is not adequate interaction in online classes and staring at screens for a long time may harm one's eyes.
  然而，与传统课程相比，网课缺乏充分的互动，而且长时间盯着屏幕可能会伤害眼睛。
- For another, compared with traditional cars, new-energy vehicles are much cheaper, more eco-friendly, more efficient and require less maintenance. Thus, an increasing number of people have chosen new-energy cars.
  另一方面，与传统汽车相比，新能源汽车更加便宜、环保、高效，需要的保养也更少。因此，越来越多的人选择了新能源汽车。

### 4. 文字说明

- Finally, three Chinese characters can be noticed below the pictures, which say/read "the power of action".
  最后，我们能注意到图画下方有三个汉字，写的是"行动力"。
  注：can be noticed 可替换为 are given。

### 5. 概述图表

- This/Presented is a table, clearly illustrating the changes in the domestic sales of gasoline-powered and new-energy vehicles in China during the first half of 2025.
  这是一张表格，清晰展示了 2025 年上半年中国燃油车和新能源汽车国内销量的变化。
  注：根据图表类型，可将 table 替换为 column chart（柱状图）、bar chart（条形图）、line graph（折线图）、pie chart（饼状图）等。

### 6. 数据上升/下降/稳定（适用于动态变化图表）

- To be specific, the number of netizens displayed/showed a sharp increase, from 688.26 million in 2015 to 1031.95 million in 2021.
  具体来讲，网民数量呈现出明显上升，从 2015 年的 6.8826 亿上升到了 2021 年的 10.3195 亿。
  注：number 可根据情况调整为 percentage/proportion/rate（比例）或 share（份额）。
  注：sharp 可调整为 significant（显著的）、moderate（适度的，中等的）等。
  注：displayed/showed a sharp increase 还可以表达为 ascended rapidly/increased remarkably。
  注：如果表示数据下降，可将 increase 改为 decline、decrease。
- Meanwhile, a chart can be noticed on the right, which clearly displays the remarkable increase in the proportion of e-book usage, from 30% in 2019 to 70% in 2023.
  与此同时，我们可以注意到右边有一张图表，清晰展示了电子书使用比例的显著增长，从 2019 年的 30% 增加到 2023 年的 70%。
- However, the percentage of paper book usage showed an opposite trend, decreasing from 70% to 30%.
  然而，纸质书的使用比例呈现出相反的趋势，从 70% 下降到 30%。
- By contrast, the sales of gasoline cars fluctuated around 1.1 million.
  相比之下，燃油车的销量在 110 万辆上下波动。

### 7. 比例排序（适用于静态图表）

- Overall, seniors have the largest proportion of part-time workers, at 88.24%. The next are juniors and sophomores, at 71.93% and 71.13% respectively. Finally come freshmen, at 67.77%.
  总体上，大四学生兼职比例最高，达到了 88.24%；紧随其后的是大三和大二学生，分别是 71.93% 和 71.13%；排在最后的是大一学生，为 67.77%。
- Overall, the expense on New Year gifts ranks first/is the largest proportion, accounting for 40%. The next is the expenditure on transportation, accounting for 30%, followed by the expenditure on dining at 20%. Finally comes the total spending on others, at 10%.
  总体上，新年礼物的花销占最大比重，占 40%。其次是交通费用，占 30%。聚餐费用紧随其后，占 20%。最后是其他花销的总和，为 10%。

### 8. 图画/图表联系主旨

- （图画）Such a scenario, apparently/evidently, can be associated with some parents' negative attitudes towards their children.
  显然，这样的场景能让人联想到一些父母对待孩子的消极态度。
  注："apparently/evidently, can be associated with" 可替换为 can naturally be associated with；associated 可替换为 connected。
- （图画+图表）Such a scene and the statistics above, apparently, can be associated with the growing popularity of e-books among youngsters in reality.
  显然，上述场景及数据能让人联想到现实中电子书越来越受年轻人欢迎。
- （图表）Such statistics can be naturally associated with college students' varied lifestyles.
  这些数据自然能让人联想到大学生各种各样的生活方式。

### 9. 普遍现象

- However, in reality, a host of youngsters tend to think about various goals, but take no action.
  然而，在现实生活中，很多年轻人往往想着各种目标却不采取行动。
  注：however 可根据情况调整为 admittedly（诚然，无可否认）、similarly（同样）。

### 10. 引出原因

- Then, what resulted in the phenomenon above/these changes? To my understanding, at least two reasons can account for this phenomenon/these changes.
  那么，出现上述现象/这些变化的原因是什么？在我看来，至少有两个原因可以解释这种现象/这些变化。

### 11. 典型特征（+结果）

- Successful individuals are characterized by their diverse excellent qualities such as diligence, confidence, perseverance, and the ability to take action.
  成功人士的特征是具有各种优秀品质，例如勤奋、自信、持之以恒和付诸行动的能力。
- For one thing, in recent years, China has been characterized by its boom and prosperity in economy, which has enabled a host of families to possess additional income to purchase gasoline-powered or new-energy cars.
  一方面，近年来，中国的典型特征在于其经济的繁荣发展，这使得很多家庭有了额外的收入来购买燃油车或新能源汽车。

### 12. 值得关注

- This phenomenon is particularly worth attention for the reason that the earth can be likened to our shared homeland which requires our care and love.
  这种现象非常值得关注，因为地球可以比作我们共同的家园，它需要我们的关心和爱护。
  注：for the reason that 可替换为 because。
- It is particularly noteworthy that the sales of new-energy vehicles surpassed those of gasoline-powered vehicles in May.
  尤其值得注意的是，新能源汽车的销量在 5 月份超过了燃油车。

### 13. 原因（只关心……，却忽视了……）

- This phenomenon is particularly worth attention for the reason that these parents focus only on their own comfort and enjoyment, ignoring the significance of communicating with their children.
  这种现象非常值得关注，因为这些父母只关心自身的舒适和快乐，却忽视了和孩子沟通的重要性。
  注：focus only on 可替换为 concentrate only on、care only about 或 just concern themselves with；ignoring 可替换为 overlooking、neglecting。

### 14. 原因/重大意义（强调句）

- It is their positive attitudes that enable them to accumulate adequate experience and skills, and to succeed again and again.
  正是积极的态度使他们能够积累足够的经验、技能，一次又一次地成功。
  注：enable 可根据情况调整为 allow。
- It is their laziness and reluctance to act that cause them to gain nothing in life, work, and study.
  正是他们的懒惰和不愿行动导致他们在生活、工作和学习中一无所获。

### 15. 积极意义（排比句）

- Numerous innovative technological products can bring us great convenience, inspire our passion for a promising life, and help us enhance work efficiency.
  众多的创新科技产品能给我们带来巨大的便利，激发我们对美好生活的热情，并帮助我们提高工作效率。
  注：inspire one's passion for 可替换为 arouse one's passion for 或 evoke one's enthusiasm for。

### 16. 危害

- As a matter of fact, this foolish behavior has constituted an obstacle that hinders young people's growth and progress.
  事实上，这种愚蠢的做法已经构成了一种障碍，阻碍年轻人的成长和进步。

### 17. 个人观点

- As a young man, I cling to the idea that/I am convinced that it is advisable for every citizen to maintain and protect the environment.
  作为一个年轻人，我坚信每个公民都应该维护和保护环境。
- As a youngster, I prefer watching health education videos and TV programs.
  作为年轻人，我喜欢观看健康教育视频和电视节目。
- In a nutshell, as a youngster, I deem that it is advisable for parents to accompany their children and communicate more with them, which can bring kids warmth, inspire their passion for life, and help them enjoy happiness and delight.
  简而言之，作为一个年轻人，我认为父母应该陪伴孩子并多与他们交流，这会带给孩子温暖，激发他们对生活的热爱，并帮助他们享受幸福和快乐。

### 18. 观点+比喻

- To my understanding, one's internal motivation can be likened to the key to success.
  在我看来，一个人的内在动力可以比作通往成功的钥匙。
  注：to my understanding 可替换为 from my perspective。

### 19. 建议

- Instead, it is of great necessity for us to enjoy in-depth travelling since it is travelling with heart that enables us to learn about different cultures, taste diverse foods, and make more friends.
  相反，我们很有必要享受深度游，因为正是用心旅行使我们得了解更多文化，品尝各种美食，结交更多朋友。
- As a college student, I deem that it is advisable for us to utilize modern technological products, but it is of no necessity to be slaves to IT products.
  作为一名大学生，我认为我们应该使用现代科技产品，但没必要完全依赖 IT 产品。
- To my understanding, China's car manufacturers are supposed to pay more attention to the new-energy car market due to its huge potential.
  在我看来，中国的汽车制造商应该更关注新能源汽车市场，因为其潜力巨大。
- Meanwhile, it is advisable for them to work out comprehensive strategies to develop more types of new-energy vehicles, such as solar-powered cars.
  同时，他们还应制定综合策略来开发更多种类的新能源汽车，比如太阳能汽车。

### 20. 号召

- Thus, everyone is supposed to bear in mind that actions speak louder than words.
  因此，每个人都应牢记行动胜于空言。
- Accordingly, society as a whole should foster a positive atmosphere to encourage residents to do good deeds in daily life and criticize those who refuse to shoulder any social obligation.
  因此，整个社会应营造一种积极的氛围，来鼓励居民在日常生活中做好事，并批评那些拒绝承担任何社会责任的人。

### 21. 举例/列举

- A case in point is myself.
  一个典型的例子就是我自己。
- A typical example is the scene/scenario in the left picture.
  左图中的场景就是一个典型的例子。
- Successful people are characterized by their diverse excellent qualities, such as optimism, confidence, and the spirit of learning from setbacks.
  成功人士的特征是具有各种优秀品质，例如乐观、自信和从挫折中学习的精神。
  注：such as 可根据情况调整为 including。
